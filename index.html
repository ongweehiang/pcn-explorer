About the data

The network combines URA Master Plan 2025 park connector lines with NParks' Park Connector Loop, NParks Tracks and the older NParks connector map. Every Master Plan line was checked against those three and against OpenStreetMap.

■ Confirmed connector — at least one other source shows it.
■ Unverified — only the Master Plan shows it, so it may still be planned. Routing avoids it slightly.
■ Link path — park paths, footpaths and bridges that join connectors (OpenStreetMap / NParks). These don't count toward your coverage.
■ Gap — a short break (usually a road crossing) that no dataset draws. Check it on the ground.

Toilets, water points, shelters and bike repair stations come from OpenStreetMap, so some may be missing or closed. Hawker centres come from NEA. Built .
